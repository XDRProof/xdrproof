## The shape

A transaction whose LIQUIDITY_POOL_WITHDRAW operation fails at apply is still written into the closed ledger: its fee is charged, its sequence number is consumed, and nothing it did is kept. The result carries the operation's code (line_full). The account's NRV sits in the pool and its NRV trustline limit was cut to one stroop: the 100 NRV a full withdrawal returns cannot land.
