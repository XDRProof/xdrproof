# A claimable balance is clawed back by the issuer (/docs/corpus/entries/claimable-balance-clawback)

Where it sits, inside `LedgerCloseMeta` (CAP-0023, CAP-0035):

- H-C9: ClawbackClaimableBalance
- H-C10: clawback of a claimable balance

## The shape

CLAWBACK_CLAIMABLE_BALANCE removes a balance that inherited CLAIMABLE_BALANCE_CLAWBACK_ENABLED; the issuer burns it.
