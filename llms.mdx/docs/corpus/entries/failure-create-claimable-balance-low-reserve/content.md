# CREATE_CLAIMABLE_BALANCE fails with CREATE_CLAIMABLE_BALANCE_LOW_RESERVE (/docs/corpus/entries/failure-create-claimable-balance-low-reserve)

Where it sits, inside `LedgerCloseMeta` (CAP-0023):

- H-C9: claimable balance whose creator cannot fund its reserve

## The shape

A transaction whose CREATE_CLAIMABLE_BALANCE operation fails at apply is still written into the closed ledger: its fee is charged, its sequence number is consumed, and nothing it did is kept. The result carries the operation's code (low_reserve). The operation source holds 10 NRV but sits at its minimum XLM balance; a claimable balance needs 0.5 XLM of reserve from its creator. Another account pays the transaction fee.
