## The shape

A transaction whose CLAWBACK_CLAIMABLE_BALANCE operation fails at apply is still written into the closed ledger: its fee is charged, its sequence number is consumed, and none of its operations change state. The result carries the operation's result code (does_not_exist). Readers that skip failed transactions lose the fee and sequence change; readers that assume a failed transaction changed something record effects that never happened.
