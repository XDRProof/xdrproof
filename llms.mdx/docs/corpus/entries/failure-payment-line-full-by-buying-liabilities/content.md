# PAYMENT fails with PAYMENT_LINE_FULL although the trustline has room: buying liabilities reserve it (/docs/corpus/entries/failure-payment-line-full-by-buying-liabilities)

Where it sits, inside `LedgerCloseMeta` (pre-cap, CAP-0003):

- H-C1: OperationResult.payment.line_full in a closed ledger
- H-C17: PAYMENT_LINE_FULL with limit 100 and balance 0: 90 of the limit is buying liabilities

## The shape

A transaction whose PAYMENT operation fails at apply is still written into the closed ledger: its fee is charged, its sequence number is consumed, and none of its operations change state. The result carries the operation's result code (line_full). Readers that skip failed transactions lose the fee and sequence change; readers that assume a failed transaction changed something record effects that never happened. The trustline limit is 100 and the balance 0, but an offer buying 90 reserves the room: receiving 11 fails.
