## The shape

A transaction whose REVOKE_SPONSORSHIP operation fails at apply is still written into the closed ledger: its fee is charged, its sequence number is consumed, and nothing it did is kept. The result carries the operation's code (low_reserve). Revoking the sponsorship of a data entry hands its 0.5 XLM reserve to the owner, who sits at its minimum balance.
