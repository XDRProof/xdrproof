# A pre-authorized-transaction signer (T…) and a hash-x signer (X…) are added (/docs/corpus/entries/signer-preauth-and-hashx-added)

Where it sits, inside `LedgerCloseMeta` (pre-cap):

## The shape

SET_OPTIONS adds a T… signer (the hash of a future transaction) and an X… signer (the sha256 of a secret).
