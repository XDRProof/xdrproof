# PAYMENT fails with PAYMENT_UNDERFUNDED although the balance covers it: selling liabilities reserve it (/docs/corpus/entries/failure-payment-underfunded-by-selling-liabilities)

Where it sits, inside `LedgerCloseMeta` (pre-cap, CAP-0003):

- H-C1: OperationResult.payment.underfunded in a closed ledger
- H-C17: PAYMENT_UNDERFUNDED with balance 100 > amount 41: 60 of it is selling liabilities

## The shape

A transaction whose PAYMENT operation fails at apply is still written into the closed ledger: its fee is charged, its sequence number is consumed, and none of its operations change state. The result carries the operation's result code (underfunded). Readers that skip failed transactions lose the fee and sequence change; readers that assume a failed transaction changed something record effects that never happened. The account holds 100 XPF but has an offer selling 60, so only 40 is available: a payment of 41 fails although the balance covers it.
