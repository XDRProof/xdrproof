# Payments to two different muxed IDs of one account in the same ledger (/docs/corpus/entries/muxed-payment-two-ids-one-account-one-ledger)

Where it sits, inside `LedgerCloseMeta` (CAP-0027):

- H-A1: two PaymentOp.destination muxed IDs over one base account in one ledger

## The shape

Two payments in one ledger reach M(bob, 100) and M(bob, 200): two muxed addresses, one base account. Readers keyed by base account must keep both IDs; readers keyed by M address must not create two accounts. wallet-backend#693 mishandled two muxed IDs of one account in one ledger.
