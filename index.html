04 · The method

Real bytes in.
A verdict out.

No RPC, no peers, no real funds. A standalone stellar-core makes the shapes, independent readers check the facts, and real builds meet them.

01 · Makeinput

Recipes and a bare stellar-core

Fixed seeds and close times drive a standalone core v30.0.0, pinned by digest. Real-network shapes are mined from the public lake.

make generatetwice from scratch
02 · Provereaders

Facts checked by independent readers

A byte-for-byte round trip, then stellar-core's own decoder and a schema walker re-read the values.

make verify · oraclecoverage
03 · Runverdicts

Real indexers and SDKs meet them

wallet-backend builds ingest corpus ledgers; JS, Go, Rust and Python builds run the vectors. Each fix shows up as a flip.

make replay · conformancefail → pass
